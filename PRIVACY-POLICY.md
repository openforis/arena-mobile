# Arena Mobile – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 November 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is collected, used, shared and protected when you use Arena Mobile ("the Application", "we", "us"), the mobile application for Android and iOS developed by the Open Foris Initiative for offline field data collection with Open Foris Arena.

By installing or using the Application you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the Application.

Arena Mobile works offline: the data you collect is stored on your device and is sent to an Arena server only when you choose to do so. When you connect to an Arena server, the data sent to it is governed by the privacy policy of that server. For the Arena server operated by the Open Foris team (the default server, <https://www.openforis-arena.org>), see the [Open Foris Arena Privacy Policy](https://github.com/openforis/arena/blob/master/PRIVACY_POLICY.md). Organizations running their own Arena server are responsible for the personal data they process and for providing their users with their own privacy policy.

## 1. Personal data we collect

### 1.1 Data you provide

- **Survey and record data**: the surveys you import and the data you enter in the records (text, numbers, codes, dates, coordinates, images, videos, audio recordings, files, etc.).
- **Server credentials**: if you connect to an Arena server, the server address, your email address and password, and the authentication token issued by the server. They are stored only on your device, in its secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain), and are sent only to that server.
- **Settings**: your preferences (e.g. language, theme, GPS and map settings), stored on your device.

No registration is needed to use the Application: you can import the demo survey or a survey file and collect data without an account.

### 1.2 Data collected through the device

The Application uses the device features below only when you use the related functions, and only after you grant the corresponding permission:

- **Location**: the precise location of the device is read only when you request it, e.g. in coordinate fields (pressing "Start"), when showing your position on the map or when navigating to a location. It is not tracked in the background.
- **Camera and microphone**: used only to take pictures, record videos or audio in the file fields of a record.
- **Photos and media**: used only to select existing images or files to attach to a record. If permitted, the location stored in the images (EXIF data) is read to keep it with the picture.
- **Bluetooth**: used only to discover and connect to external GNSS (GPS) receivers that you choose to use.
- **Motion sensors**: used only to show the compass direction or the device orientation in the Application.

### 1.3 Data collected automatically

- **Application logs**: technical events and errors are recorded in log files stored on your device, to help troubleshoot problems. They are never sent automatically: you can choose to share them (e.g. with the Open Foris support team) from the Application.
- **Usage data**: the Application does not use analytics, advertising or crash reporting services, and does not track how you use it.

### 1.4 Personal data contained in survey data

Surveys may collect personal data about third parties (e.g. interviewees, landowners, field staff), including names, contact details, photographs or geographic locations. The survey owner (the person or organization that created and manages the survey) decides what data is collected and why, and is responsible for having a valid legal basis for collecting it, informing the data subjects and limiting the collection to what is necessary. As a data collector, follow the instructions of the survey owner when collecting and handling this data.

## 2. How we use personal data

Personal data is used only to:

- provide the Application features: data entry, validation, maps, import and export of surveys and records;
- connect to the Arena server you choose, sign in and keep you signed in, and download and upload surveys and records;
- troubleshoot problems, when you share the Application logs with us.

Personal data is not sold, and it is not used for advertising or profiling.

## 3. Sharing of personal data and third-party services

Data leaves your device only in the following cases:

- **Arena server**: when you sign in, or download or upload surveys and records, the data is exchanged with the Arena server you configured. The data you upload becomes visible to the users of that survey according to the permissions granted by the survey owner.
- **Exports and sharing**: when you export records, surveys or logs, they are shared only with the destination you choose (e.g. a file, an email or a cloud storage app).
- **Map services**: when you use the map features, the Application requests map tiles or imagery from the selected map provider (e.g. Google Maps, OpenStreetMap, OpenTopoMap, Esri). These providers receive the coordinates of the area being viewed and technical data such as your IP address. When you open a location in an external navigation app, the coordinates are passed to that app.
- **Version checks**: the Application may contact the Google Play Store or the Apple App Store to check whether a new version is available, and GitHub to show the list of changes.
- **Legal obligations**: personal data may be disclosed when required by law.

Third-party services process data according to their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to read.

## 4. Data security

Data is stored in the private storage of the Application on your device. Credentials and authentication tokens are kept in the secure storage of the device, and the communication with the Arena server uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

You are responsible for protecting your device (e.g. with a screen lock) and for uploading or exporting the collected data in time, to avoid losing it if the device is lost or damaged.

## 5. Data retention and deletion

- **Survey and record data** are kept on your device until you delete them. You can delete surveys and records at any time from the Surveys list and the Records list.
- **Credentials** are kept until you sign out (you can choose to keep only your email address).
- **Application logs** are rotated and only the most recent ones are kept.
- All the data of the Application is deleted from your device when you uninstall it.

Data uploaded to an Arena server is kept on that server according to its own policy. On the default server operated by the Open Foris team, you can delete it from the Arena web application or ask for its deletion (see section 9).

## 6. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may access, correct, export or delete your personal data. As the data is stored on your device, you can do so directly in the Application. You can stop all collection of data by the Application at any time by revoking its permissions in the device settings or by uninstalling it.

For personal data uploaded to an Arena server, contact the operator of that server; requests concerning personal data contained in a survey should be addressed first to the owner of that survey, who controls that data.

## 7. Children

The Application is intended for professional, scientific and institutional use and is not directed to children.

## 8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time, for example to reflect changes in the Application or in applicable requirements. The updated version is published here with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Application after an update means that you accept the revised policy.

## 9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or about the processing of your personal data, or to exercise your rights, contact the Open Foris team through the Open Foris support forum: <https://openforis.support>.
